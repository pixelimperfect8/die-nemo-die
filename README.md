# Die, Nemo, Die

A top-down pixel-art survival game. You are a very small clownfish at the bottom of a dark sea. Sharks want you. Last 60 seconds.

## Play

**[Play it here](https://pixelimperfect8.github.io/die-nemo-die/)**

Or open `index.html` in any browser. The whole game is one self-contained file, so you can also just email it to someone.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow keys or WASD | Swim |
| Drag | Swim, on touch screens |
| Enter or Space | Start, on the menus |

## How it works

Survive 60 seconds without touching a shark. One bite ends the run. The sharks get faster and spawn more often as the clock runs down, and the rarer species only show up once things have heated up. They all turn slowly relative to their speed, so cutting across a shark's path beats swimming away from it in a straight line.

Four species hunt you:

| Species | Build | Behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reef shark | Medium | The baseline threat, around from the first second |
| Mako | Small and thin | Fastest, turns hardest |
| Hammerhead | Wide head plate | Slightly slower, but corners well |
| Great white | Huge | Slow and clumsy, but the hitbox is enormous |

Your best time is saved in your browser.
